# bit_holder_body

Cylindrical body for 20 driver bits. Slots are arranged as 1 center slot,
7 inner-ring slots, and 12 outer-ring slots. Each slot is 7.2 mm diameter and
76 mm deep for a 6.5 mm x 75 mm bit.

The top 5 mm is a reduced 62 mm diameter neck. The matching lid uses a
62.7 mm inner recess for 0.7 mm diametral clearance.
